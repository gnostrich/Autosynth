
## Counterpoint v4: one flow, realization within the region (2026-07-11)
Listener verdict on v2/v3: "indistinguishable homogenised sludgy sound."
Diagnosis — independent channel walks destroy the two things the corpus
says channels MUST share:
1. RESTS: the per-voice presence term (an anti-death fix for solo voices)
   steers every walk toward material where its channel is LOUD — eight
   always-on channels = no entries/exits = wall of sound.
2. CLOCK: eight independent cursors = eight drifting beat grids = smear.
v4: the joint walk picks the region (a measured CONFIGURATION of all
channels, including which ones rest); each channel reader chooses its own
window WITHIN that sovereign region (own presence/flux — material may
come from different tracks pooled by the region); one shared cursor
(median native stride of the step's windows). Configuration and clock are
shared; material is free. Entries/exits emerge from the region's own
channel content. The per-channel landscapes + vertical/lagged traces
remain the right objects for ANALYSIS (set grammar, novel-move
measurement) even where ensemble SYNTHESIS uses the joint flow.
