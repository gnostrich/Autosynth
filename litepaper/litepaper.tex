\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The Equilibrium Tape Synth (ETS) learns a \emph{frozen ``world''} from $N$ real
music tracks and lets a performer \emph{play} a causal writer through six control
lanes. The world is not a sample generator: it is a single free-energy functional
$F$ whose equilibria are the real tracks, fit by noise-contrastive estimation
against the tracks' own disarrangements. The output is a \emph{settled tape}, a
progressively clamped $(N{+}1)$-th boundary node of the same hypergraph as the
input tracks, whose every emitted sample is a real source unit at a real metrical
slot with full provenance. Control never edits the world: it enters as one
exponential tilt on gauge-invariant arrangement statistics, the Doob conditioning
induced by clamping the panel as a boundary measure, with knob scales derived from
equilibrium fluctuations. The system's distinctive contribution is a \emph{method}
for building a faithful instrument: a two-state invariant manifest (14 of 15
invariants enforced by executable, non-vacuous checks), an adversarial
builder/auditor pair, prereg-commit-before-run, and an append-only registry in
which \emph{walls are information} and \emph{silent divergence is the breach
class}. We lead with the architecture, give the Gibbs energy-based model its due as
the centerpiece, and state plainly what is real versus pending --- including a
headline dissociation gate that \emph{did not pass} at v0 and is reported as-is
rather than retuned.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item a single free-energy functional $F$ over \emph{unit-resolved} couplings whose
  low-energy configurations are real tracks, fit by convex logistic NCE against a
  fixed, pre-registered family of self-generated negatives (no external data);
\item an exact, learned-content-free control law --- a Gibbs tilt whose per-lane
  scales are \emph{derived} from equilibrium fluctuations (a
  fluctuation--dissipation identity), so the six-lane panel adds no free constant;
\item a \emph{faithfulness discipline} that is the real deliverable: one authority
  ($F$), a two-state (enforced/pending) invariant manifest with checks proven to
  bite, an adversarial auditor, and an append-only registry that records a caught
  fidelity breach and a failed headline gate as first-class results.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Architecture overview (read this first)}

ETS is a pipeline from real audio to a settled tape. Each stage has a plain-terms
job and a formal object; the design's whole point is that \emph{exactly one stage
makes value judgments} --- the functional $F$ --- and every other stage measures,
transports, applies, or reports. Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline} is the labelled flow.

There are two orthogonal control paths, and only two: (i) the six \textbf{CV
lanes} enter the writer through a \emph{single jack}, the h-transform tilt (I-1);
(ii) \textbf{clamps} --- committed tape, ``this unit at bar 33'', and knob leans
as clamped role-space mass --- are all the same boundary-condition species (I-7,
generalized by the connector). No third intervention mechanism exists. The
desktop runtime is \emph{two processes} (\S6): an \textsc{engine} (Python core,
streaming writer, audio) and a \textsc{panel} (PySide6/Qt) over OSC on localhost;
killing the panel never touches the engine, and any OSC/MIDI hardware can replace
the panel.

\section{Ingestion, gauge, and the frozen world}

\paragraph{Ingestion.} Audio is resampled to 44.1\,kHz, beat-tracked
(\code{beat\_this==1.1.0}) to a tatum/beat/bar grid, and decomposed by a fixed
8-band log-spaced filterbank --- deliberately \emph{not} a separator, because
``a separator is a second authority pre-deciding roles before $F$ does'' (spec
\S2.3). The raised-cosine masks form a \emph{partition of unity}
(\code{ingestion/filterbank.py}: \code{N\_FFT=2048}, \code{HOP=512},
\code{FMIN=40\,Hz}); the per-band ISTFTs sum back to the input to numerical
precision, which makes the G0 reconstruction identity a coverage/scheduling test
rather than a lossy-model test.

\paragraph{Gauge (spec \S3).} Per track the gauge group is
transposition~$\times$~beat-phase~$\times$~loudness~$\times$~timbre-basis. All
cross-track communication is invariant under independent per-track gauge action;
\emph{no coordinate crosses a track boundary} (I-2: descriptor arrays are private,
the sole combiner refuses a cross-track pair, pitch-class cost is
transposition-quotiented, metrical cost is circular). Microtiming deviation is
\emph{not} gauge --- it is intrinsic groove content that must survive, and it is
exactly what $T_1$'s phase charge later prices.

\paragraph{Frozen world (self-sizing anchors, spec \S4).} All cross-track traffic
factors through free-support barycenter \emph{anchors} in role space; there is no
direct pairwise track coupling. Roles are compared across tracks by a
Gromov--Wasserstein distance~\cite{memoli2011gw,peyre2019cot}, which is defined
between metric-measure spaces and so needs no shared coordinate system --- exactly
the property I-2 requires. The anchor count is not a hyperparameter --- it is
\emph{derived} as the balanced-truncation effective rank (participation ratio
$(\sum w)^2/\sum w^2$) of the cross-track GW role-affinity operator
$e^{-D_{\mathrm{role}}/\sigma}$ (\code{anchors.py:effective\_rank}). On the v0
world this is $M=5$ (eff\_rank $\approx 5.354$, $\sigma \approx 0.553$). The
supports $(D,a,B,\theta)$ are then settled as the GW barycenter at that count.
Anchors hold \emph{only} support and mass --- no accumulator, EMA, or momentum
(I-3, structurally scanned).

\emph{Honest note.} The effective-rank rule is a \emph{re-derivation after a wall}:
the first sizing rule (a GW transport-residual growth criterion) provably failed
to dissociate role-diverse from same-role corpora and grew with $N$, and was
rejected; the current rule reads the spec's own ``McMillan degree of \emph{traffic}
/ Hankel mass / balanced truncation'' language as a spectral rank
(\code{RETRO\_AUDIT.md}). The wall was reported and the object re-posed, not
patched.

\section{The Gibbs energy-based model (centerpiece)}

The heart of ETS is a \emph{single free-energy functional} $F$ (invariant I-4:
there is no training loss distinct from $F$). Framed correctly, ETS is an
\textbf{energy-based model}~\cite{lecun2006tutorial,du2019implicit}: the energy is
$F$, the sampling looseness is a \emph{temperature} $T_s$, and control is an
\emph{exponential tilt} in sufficient statistics $\varphi$. Real tracks are the
low-energy equilibria; their rearrangements are not.

\subsection{$F$ over the unit-resolved coupling}

$F$ ranges over couplings $\pi$ (unit $\rightarrow$ role $\rightarrow$ metrical
slot), channel gains $B$, anchor supports/masses $(D,a)$, and gauge sections $g$.
Crucially, \emph{$F$ is posed on the full unit-resolved $\pi$}, not on its
role-occupancy marginal $O[k,s]=\sum_{\text{units}}\pi$. A term may use the
marginal \emph{only where it provably factors through it}; a term that needs the
fiber (which real unit sits where) reads $\pi$ directly (Table~\ref{tab:terms}).

The \textbf{phase-displacement charge} is what makes groove cost something. For
each placed unit it charges the circular distance between the unit's
\emph{intrinsic} metrical coordinate $x_u$ and the phase of the slot it occupies,
quotiented by a single per-section global phase shift $\delta$ (a global
beat-phase shift is free gauge; a per-unit displacement is charged). The
$\delta$-quotient is solved in closed form,
\begin{equation}
  \min_{\delta}\ \sum_u m_u\big(1-\cos 2\pi(x_u-\delta)\big)
  \;=\; 1-\Big|\textstyle\sum_u m_u\, e^{\,i 2\pi x_u}\Big|
  \qquad(\text{masses normalized}),
  \label{eq:phase}
\end{equation}
which is exactly $0$ when every unit sits at its own intrinsic slot up to one
global shift (a real track's groove) and strictly positive for incoherent
displacement. $T_4$ rewards output-adjacent slots holding genuine \emph{source}
successors: a grid-shuffle re-deals content so almost no adjacency survives; a
cross-track graft inserts units that are no track's successor, so the run breaks.
Both fiber terms are gauge-invariant by construction (I-2). Holonomy, drift,
novelty, and any meter appear \emph{nowhere} in $F$ (I-5, I-14; enforced by an AST
scan that bites on a \code{holonomy\_drift} identifier and on any \code{ets.meters}
import in the $F$ path).

\subsection{The Gibbs measure and the Layer-0 tilt}

The equilibrium measure over an arrangement $a$, with control applied, is
\begin{equation}
  p(a)\ \propto\ \exp\!\Big(-\tfrac{F(a)}{T_s} + \sum_i \lambda_i\,\varphi_i(a)\Big),
  \qquad \lambda_i = \frac{u_i}{\sigma_{\varphi_i}}
  \qquad(\text{connector Layer 0}).
  \label{eq:gibbs}
\end{equation}
Here $T_s$ is the \emph{temperature} (sampling looseness around the settled
optimum; it carries no $\varphi$); the $\varphi_i$ are five gauge-invariant
arrangement statistics (region, density, continuity, gauge-move magnitude,
novelty) computable from the candidate arrangement alone (\code{connector/phi.py}).
The tilt is not a bolt-on: it is the \emph{Doob conditioning} the settlement
inherits when the panel's boundary measure is clamped. Two of the five $\varphi$
(region, density) are linear in $O$ and enter the O-block; continuity and novelty
live on the fiber block; gauge on the (v0-frozen) gauge block. Run-time knobs move
$\lambda$ and $T_s$, never $F$'s term weights (I-9).

\subsection{Contrastive (NCE) training --- and the honest KILL}

The weights \code{LAMBDA} are fit \emph{once}, at corpus time, by a convex
logistic NCE~\cite{gutmann2010nce} (spec \S6, \code{training/nce.py}). The
condition is: \emph{each real track is an equilibrium of $F$; its rearrangements
are not}. There is \emph{no
external ``bad music'' data anywhere} (I-6): the comparison class is generated
internally by disarranging each real track's own units, drawn only from a fixed,
pre-registered scramble family (Table~\ref{tab:scramble}). Because $F$ is linear in
the weights and $\varphi$ is computed at the frozen \code{LAMBDA}-free world, the
NCE objective is \emph{convex} with an exact gradient --- no circularity. The fit
uses seeds $\{1,2,3\}$; held-out validity is measured on \emph{disjoint} seeds
$\{4,5\}$, so no fit metric is a gate metric (I-5).

\subsection{Settlement as certified I-projections}

Minimization is block-coordinate I-projections (\code{functional/solver.py}):
Sinkhorn~\cite{cuturi2013sinkhorn} on $\pi$-blocks, exponentiated-gradient (mirror
descent) on $B$-blocks, unbalanced multiplicative updates on masses, a closed-form
Gromov--Wasserstein barycenter~\cite{peyre2016gw} on $D$, exact minimization on the
gauge block. The certificate is a \emph{Lyapunov
$F$-descent guard}: every block step is accepted \emph{only} if $F$ does not
increase, so the $F$-trajectory is monotone non-increasing and the run stops at
$|\Delta F| < \text{tol}$. The accept/reject decision reads $F$ and nothing else
--- no second objective, no meter (I-4/I-14, verified by parsing \code{batch\_solve}
and confirming the guard compares $F_{\mathrm{cand}}/F_{\mathrm{cur}}$). In
streaming mode the batch Lyapunov certificate becomes a per-step frontier
$F$-descent plus a \emph{bounded-state} certificate: state growth on stationary
input is a broken instrument --- halt and report (I-8).

\paragraph{The knob-scaling law is derived, not hand-set.} Each lane's scale is
$\lambda_i = u_i/\sigma_{\varphi_i}$, where $\sigma_{\varphi_i}$ is the
\emph{equilibrium fluctuation} of $\varphi_i$ under the \emph{untilted} writer,
measured by a registered calibration pass at world-freeze
(\code{calibration/sigma\_phi.json}). The derivation is
fluctuation--dissipation: with $p_u \approx p_0\, e^{\lambda\varphi}$ one has
$d\langle\varphi\rangle/d\lambda|_0 = \mathrm{Var}_0(\varphi)$, so
$\lambda = u/\sigma$ gives $d\langle\varphi\rangle/du|_0 = \sigma$ --- \emph{one
knob unit $=$ one equilibrium-$\sigma$ lean}. $\sigma$ is the unique normalizer
with this property and makes the tilt invariant to any rescaling of $\varphi$. No
hand-set $\lambda$ scale exists in the runtime. The calibration ensemble was 7495
bars (the corpus bar count --- the evidence scale, not a hand constant),
$s_{\text{phase}}=8$.

\paragraph{Two lanes are honestly disarmed at v0.} The untilted writer settles by a
deterministic MAP descent on a bar-periodic anchor field, so $\varphi_{\text{density}}$
(an $O$-marginal) is \emph{pinned} constant ($\sigma=0$ exactly), and the single
identity-gauge section makes $\varphi_{\text{gauge}}\equiv 0$. Rather than invent a
floor, the loader marks these lanes \code{identifiable=false}: $u$ still transmits,
but no tilt is applied and the engine surfaces the state (\code{WorldNotCalibrated}
on a lean into an uncalibrated world). This is a theorem about degenerate
exponential families reported as a wall, not an error-hiding branch.

\paragraph{Two processes over OSC (spec \S12).} The \textsc{engine}
(\code{ets/engine}) owns the frozen world, the streaming writer, and audio
(\code{sounddevice}, lazy, headless-graceful). The \textsc{panel}
(\code{ets/panel}, PySide6/Qt) is a pure control surface: six lanes, XY vector pad,
meter jacks as LEDs, patch-cable feedback metaphor, MIDI CC learn. IPC is OSC over
localhost (\code{python-osc}); no web technology anywhere in the runtime (I-13,
enforced by an import scan that even distinguishes the runtime's own
\code{ets.panel} from the forbidden HoloViz \code{panel}). Control latency is a
\emph{declared} $L$ bars measured by buffer math on the host (desktop: $L=2$ bars,
$T_{\text{prod}}$ mean $0.568$\,s $< T_{\text{bar}}\,1.486$\,s;
\code{latency\_desktop.json}); an underrun inside $L\cdot T_{\text{bar}}$ is a WALL
--- there is no degraded-quality fallback.

\paragraph{Meter jacks.} Read-only, never in any objective/gradient/settlement
(I-5). The conflated DRIFT jack was split into two shadow meters --- \code{slide}
(per-bar displacement of the settled gauge frame, in $F$'s own quotient) and
\code{loop} (a per-committed-bar transplant of the G2 loop-defect integrator) ---
then the old conflated jack was \emph{deleted} after a registered one-shot
regression showed it is exactly informationless on every producible trace (residual
$0.0$ at machine precision, registry \code{conflation-regression-stage1}).

\section{The faithfulness discipline (the project's identity)}

ETS treats faithfulness as an engineering surface with teeth, not a virtue.

\paragraph{Two-state invariant manifest} (\code{tests/invariants/manifest.py}).
Every invariant I-1..I-15 carries exactly one of two statuses --- \textsc{enforced}
(an executable check that raises on violation) or \textsc{pending} (the guarded
feature does not exist yet). There is \emph{no third state}: \textsc{pending} never
means ``we chose not to check,'' and no invariant may be absent or vacuously
satisfied. When a feature lands, its invariant moves to \textsc{enforced} \emph{in
the same change} or the auditor rejects the diff. Current state: \textbf{14 of 15
enforced}; only \textbf{I-10 (thin planner) is pending}, because the planner is not
built. The checks are aggressively non-vacuous --- each proves it \emph{bites}
against a planted mutant (a choosing render, an accumulator field, an
$\eta{\cdot}$KL tether, a web import, an EOC-forked settlement guard).
Table~\ref{tab:inv} lists all fifteen.

\paragraph{Builder/auditor adversarial pair.} Implementation is done by an
\code{ets-builder} agent and must be paired with a read-only \code{ets-auditor};
no gate runs and no merge happens without an auditor PASS. The auditor reports
verdicts and never fixes code.

\paragraph{Prereg-commit-before-run, append-only registry.} Every gate's
hypothesis, procedure, null, and kill condition is appended to \code{PREREG.md} and
committed \emph{before} the run; \code{REGISTRY.jsonl} is append-only. Corrections
are new entries, never edits.

\paragraph{Operating slogan.} \emph{Walls are information, patches are sabotage,
silent divergence is the breach class.} A wall (a failed gate, a non-identifiable
scale, an unmeetable deadline) is surfaced and owned; the failure mode the
discipline exists to catch is a \emph{silent} divergence between what the spec
defines and what the code does.

\subsection*{Case study --- the fidelity breach (the discipline working)}

The most instructive event in the build is a \emph{fidelity breach caught before
delivery} (registry \code{fidelity-breach-2026-07-13}). An early implementation
silently collapsed the unit-resolved coupling $\pi$ to its marginal $O$ --- a
structure-deleting conversion shim. The spec defines $\pi$ as unit-resolved
(\S2/\S5); the implementation aggregated without presenting the divergence. It was
caught not by luck but by the \emph{pre-registered exam}: the fixed scramble family
correctly refused to separate (grid-shuffle held-out $\approx 0.35$, negative
margin), the KILL condition fired, and --- critically --- no \code{LAMBDA} was
emitted and the wall was surfaced pre-delivery. Remediation: (1) $F$ re-posed on
the full fiber; (2) a new invariant \textbf{I-15} with two teeth --- a term-input
contract in \code{f.py} behaviorally verified against an $O$-preserving $\pi$
rearrangement, and a referee-non-degeneracy check that the scored corpus-time
feature \emph{must} consume a unit-resolved fiber. The incident is logged
\code{logged-permanent-no-relitigation}. This makes the paper \emph{stronger}: the
mechanism meant to catch an unfaithful implementation did, and left a durable guard
behind.

\section{Related work}

ETS is a \emph{conjunction} of established primitives, and its positioning claim is
deliberately narrow: the novelty is the combination and the discipline, not any one
mechanism. We state the prior art plainly (mirroring the project's own
``occupancy ledger,'' spec \S16).

\paragraph{Corpus-based and concatenative synthesis.} Assembling output from real
segmented source units, chosen by descriptor proximity, is the concatenative /
corpus-based tradition --- CataRT and the surrounding
survey~\cite{schwarz2006catart,schwarz2007corpus}. ETS shares the ``play real
grains'' commitment but differs in what \emph{chooses}: not nearest-neighbour in a
descriptor space but the equilibrium of a single functional, with placement
carrying settled mass and full provenance. Mashup systems such as
AutoMashUpper~\cite{davies2014automashupper} pre-decide alignment by
harmonic/rhythmic ``mashability''; ETS instead routes all cross-track traffic
through self-sized anchors and lets $F$, not a similarity score, set the coupling.

\paragraph{Energy-based models and contrastive estimation.} The energy-as-model
framing and its training are classical~\cite{lecun2006tutorial,du2019implicit};
noise-contrastive estimation~\cite{gutmann2010nce} is our estimator, specialized so
that the negatives are the corpus's \emph{own} disarrangements (no external data,
I-6) and the fit is convex in the weights. Unlike a typical deep EBM, $F$ here is a
transparent optimal-transport free energy with named terms, and no run-time control
edits its weights (I-9).

\paragraph{Optimal transport for audio and music.} Sinkhorn-regularized
OT~\cite{cuturi2013sinkhorn,peyre2019cot} and Gromov--Wasserstein
matching/averaging~\cite{memoli2011gw,peyre2016gw} are the computational backbone of
the world and the settlement. OT has been used directly on audio, e.g.\ spectral
transport for portamento/morphing~\cite{henderson2019audiotransport}; ETS uses OT
one level up --- on beat-clocked \emph{unit clouds} and their role geometry --- and
turns the transport plan into a schedule of real units rather than an interpolated
spectrum.

\paragraph{Neural audio codecs and controllable synthesis.} Contemporary systems
tokenize audio with learned codecs~\cite{zeghidour2021soundstream,defossez2022encodec}
and generate with language models over those tokens~\cite{copet2023musicgen}, or
control synthesis through differentiable DSP~\cite{engel2020ddsp}. ETS occupies a
different point: it has \emph{no decoder and no learned readout} --- the tape node's
coupling \emph{is} the output and the render only applies it (I-11/I-12) --- and its
single learned object is the frozen scalar-weight vector \code{LAMBDA}, everything
else being derived mechanism or read-only instrument. The trade is explicit: ETS
buys faithfulness and provenance at the cost of the open-ended generative range a
codec-plus-LM enjoys.

\paragraph{G2 in detail (the headline that did not land).} G2 tests whether the
frozen world carries \emph{holonomy} (loop-defect curvature) above a
residual-conditioned null --- the dissociation that would support the conjecture
that musical structure is real geometric curvature, at a demanding $20\times$
target. It \textbf{failed} (\code{g2\_results.json}): real median curvature came in
\emph{below} the null ($\kappa_{\text{real}}/\kappa_{\text{null}}\approx0.45$),
dominance over null p95/p99 both false, \code{signal\_present\_above\_null=false}.
Real curvature \emph{did} clear the solver floor
($\kappa_{\text{real}}/\kappa_{\text{floor}}\approx2.0$), but not the
residual-conditioned null. Per the pre-registered kill condition this is reported
as-is, with no null-swap and no retune. Two consequences stated plainly:
(i)~the strong ``curvature $=$ structure'' claim is \emph{unsupported at v0};
(ii)~the instrument survives as a read-only meter (\code{loop}) regardless. The
pairwise-blindness and closed-loop \emph{form} theses (G3--G6) depend on the
planner, which is not built (I-10 pending), so they remain untested.

\section{Conclusion}

ETS is an instrument whose ``world'' is a single Gibbs free energy fit so that real
tracks are its equilibria, played by a causal writer that clamps its own frontier
in real time and steered by an exact exponential tilt on gauge-invariant statistics
whose knob scales are derived from equilibrium fluctuations. Its output is a settled
tape of real source units, provenance-complete, not a synthesized sample. Its
distinctive contribution is a \emph{method}: one authority ($F$), two-state
invariants with executable non-vacuous checks, an adversarial auditor, and an
append-only registry where a caught fidelity breach and a failed headline gate are
recorded as first-class results. The honest ledger --- 14/15 invariants enforced,
two lanes disarmed, one greedy realization, a failed G2, a pending planner --- is
not an apology; it is the artifact.
