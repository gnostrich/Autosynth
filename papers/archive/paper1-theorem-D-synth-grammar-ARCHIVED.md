(ii) *Instance level:* within a type (e.g. several T1 scalars), the human

pair (that observable's force = the knob; its value = the meter). A gesture
word that fails to pin a unique conjugate observable is a detectable design

(iii) *Canonical rendering:* a conjugate pair is honestly displayed as two

control families (many conjugate pairs at once) compress to the observable
